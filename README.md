# 🧠 Media Monitor AI

**AI-powered media intelligence assistant for monitoring, analyzing, and understanding information flows across Russian-language news sources.**

Media Monitor AI helps analysts, journalists, researchers, communications teams, and decision-makers transform large volumes of news content into actionable insights.

Instead of manually reviewing dozens of publications, users can instantly understand how a topic is being covered, what sentiment dominates the conversation, and how media attention evolves over time.

---

## The Problem

Modern information environments generate thousands of news articles every day.

For analysts and researchers, answering simple questions often requires reviewing dozens of sources:

* What are media outlets saying about a topic?
* Is the overall coverage positive, negative, or neutral?
* Which narratives dominate the discussion?
* How does media attention change over time?
* Which sources are driving the conversation?

This process is time-consuming and difficult to scale.

Media Monitor AI automates media monitoring and transforms raw news streams into structured intelligence.

---

## Key Features

### 🔍 Cross-Source News Monitoring

Search and aggregate relevant articles from multiple Russian-language news sources in real time.

```bash
/monitor economy
/monitor elections
/monitor energy
/monitor inflation
```

### 📊 Sentiment Intelligence

Automatically analyzes coverage and calculates:

* Positive sentiment share
* Negative sentiment share
* Neutral sentiment share
* Overall sentiment score
* Visual sentiment indicators

This allows users to quickly assess the emotional tone surrounding a topic.

### 📰 Rapid News Discovery

Retrieve the latest headlines and articles for any monitored topic.

```bash
/news economy
/news technology
/news transportation
```

### 📚 Source Transparency

View all monitored sources and understand where insights originate.

```bash
/sources
```
